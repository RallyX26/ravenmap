AIM YOUR CAMERA

This page points a camera at the road it actually watches.

Open it from the device that registered the camera and it finds the key by itself. Otherwise paste the camera token you were given when you registered it.

This camera is registered as carried (driving mode). A carried camera has no watched road, so there is nothing to aim — its sightings carry their own position.

Drag from the camera toward the road it watches. That sets the direction and how far it can see, in one movement.
