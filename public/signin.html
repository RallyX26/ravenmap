Sign in to your camera

Nothing was deleted. Your camera, its history and its watched road are all still here — this browser just lost the key that opens it. Paste the key and you are back in.

Where is my key? It is the link you copied from the camera page (Copy the key link), the QR you saved, or the key file on a USB stick. Anyone who has it has the camera, so keep it somewhere only you can reach.

Really lost it? There is no reset — the key is the only credential and we hold no email or account to prove it was yours. Set the camera up again from the camera page and it will start a fresh node. Its old sightings stay on the map under the old camera.

Looking for the review page instead? That takes a reviewer token, not this key.
